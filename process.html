Delivery Process

Four stages to resolved

01
Initial Consultation
We review your environment and objectives to determine the appropriate technical approach. The focus is on understanding scope and operational context.
02
Scoping and Planning
Defined scope, structured pricing, and a delivery plan aligned to operational requirements and risk profile.
03

Senior led delivery executed directly within your environment with clear communication throughout each stage.

Engagement Models

Flexible structures for every need

Pricing and engagement structure are aligned to operational requirements after the initial consultation.

Hourly
Ideal for targeted troubleshooting, incident triage, or advisory support. Scoped per session with defined objectives and outcome documentation.
Best for: Incidents · Advisory · Targeted work
Fixed Scope
Defined deliverables, timeline, and pricing agreed upfront. Best suited for migrations, deployments, and structured project work with clear success criteria.
Best for: Migrations · Deployments · Projects
Retainer
Ongoing access to senior engineering support on a scheduled or as-needed basis. Suitable for operational support, patch management, and continuous oversight.
Best for: Operations · Ongoing support · Oversight
