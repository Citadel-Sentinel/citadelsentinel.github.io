Citadel Sentinel IT & Cybersecurity Solutions Inc. is a specialized infrastructure consulting firm based in Milton, Ontario, serving organizations across the Greater Toronto Area and Canada.

We help IT leaders and operations teams resolve infrastructure instability, critical system failures, and outages that impact business continuity, compliance, and service delivery. Our clients operate in high-stakes environments — healthcare, financial services, and regulated industries — where systems must perform reliably.

Founded by Saboor Ali, a CCNP-certified DevOps Engineer with deep enterprise IT experience spanning professional services, infrastructure security, and critical communications systems. Citadel Sentinel operates as a lean, expert-led consultancy. No layers of management. No junior technicians. Direct access to senior expertise on every engagement.

We work on a project, hourly, or managed retainer basis — structured around your operational needs and timeline, with emergency slots available when systems go down.

RightFax On-Premises to Azure Cloud Migration
Cloud Migration

Led the full lifecycle migration of a financial institution's on-premises RightFax infrastructure to Microsoft Azure. Designed network and systems architecture, built out high-availability Azure infrastructure including load balancing, SQL integration, and network security controls. Integrated RightFax with MuleSoft via SDK, deployed the RightFax Web Portal via IIS, and concluded with RightFax Connect implementation — eliminating physical fax infrastructure entirely.

RightFax DiD SIP Trunk Migration — 7-Phase
Telecom Migration

Managed and executed a complex 7-phase RightFax DiD SIP migration, transitioning from legacy PBX to a modern SIP trunk over three months. Each phase underwent formal CAB approval with ServiceNow change management. Coordinated directly with the telecom provider for DiD number porting, validated all configurations in a dev environment before production, and performed end-to-end testing after each phase. Concluded with full decommission of the legacy fax gateway.
