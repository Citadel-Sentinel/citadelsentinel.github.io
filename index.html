About

Infrastructure Stability and Critical Systems Support

Citadel Sentinel IT and Cybersecurity Solutions Inc. is a specialized infrastructure consulting firm based in Milton, Ontario, serving organizations across the Greater Toronto Area.

We support IT leaders and operations teams in maintaining and improving enterprise infrastructure environments where reliability, continuity, and operational performance are essential.

We work across healthcare, financial services, and regulated industries, supporting enterprise systems that require structured troubleshooting and controlled change management.

Founded by Saboor Ali, a CCNP certified RightFax SME with experience across enterprise infrastructure, professional services, security operations, and critical communications systems. Citadel Sentinel operates as a lean, senior led consultancy with direct engagement throughout delivery.

Engagements are delivered on a project, hourly, or retainer basis aligned to operational requirements. Support is available for production incidents requiring structured triage and stabilization.
